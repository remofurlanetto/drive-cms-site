Privacy policy

Drive CMS. Last updated 14 November 2026.

What the application is

Drive CMS is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. It edits and publishes websites whose content is kept in your Google Drive.

Google data it uses

  • Your e-mail address, to show which account is signed in.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets files of your sites: the site folders you open or create in the application, to show and change their pages and settings, and to build the published site.

It does not read other files of your Drive for any other purpose, and it does not use your Google data for advertising.

Where your data goes

  • The application talks directly to Google's APIs from your computer. There is no server of ours in between, and no Google data is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your sign-in tokens are kept only on your computer, in the application's folder. Signing out or deleting that folder removes them; you can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • What you choose to publish (the pages of your site) is uploaded to the static host you configure, such as GitHub Pages, and is public there by your choice.

Sharing and sale

Your Google data is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties. Its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write to the support e-mail shown on the Google sign-in screen of the application.
